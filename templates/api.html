Use the data

Everything on this site is plain JSON files you can read from your own pages and tools. No key, no sign-up. The source code and the full dataset are on GitHub.

Endpoints

Base address: https://speeches.labs.trlibrary.com/api/v1/

FileWhat it holds
speeches.jsonOne row per speech: id, date, title, place id, kind, topics, word count, and whether it has full text or an archive record.
speeches/{id}.jsonOne speech in full: text by paragraph, sources (Theodore Roosevelt Center, Library of Congress, printed edition), place with coordinates, topics, people and places he named, and word statistics.
on-this-day/{MM-DD}.jsonSpeeches given on a calendar date, in every year.
places.jsonEach place he spoke, with coordinates and the ids of its speeches.
themes.jsonTopics, their descriptions, and counts by year.
phrases.jsonPhrases found in many speeches.
words.jsonMost-used words overall and by year, and words spoken per year.
people.json, mentioned-places.jsonPeople and places he named, with the speeches that name them.
corpus.jsonEvery full text in one file (about 5 MB), for search tools.

Example

fetch("https://speeches.labs.trlibrary.com/api/v1/on-this-day/10-14.json")
  .then(r => r.json())
  .then(d => d.speeches.forEach(s => console.log(s.date, s.title)));

Terms

The speech texts are in the public domain. The links, dates, places and topics are free to use; please credit the Theodore Roosevelt Presidential Library and link back. Catalog descriptions written by the Theodore Roosevelt Center are not copied here; follow the links to read them on its site.

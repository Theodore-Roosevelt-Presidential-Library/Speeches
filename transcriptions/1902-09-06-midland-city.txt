I thank you very much indeed, ladies and gentlemen.   I am coming back to Ohio in two or three weeks.   On this trip I am simply going down to the convention of the Brotherhood of Locomotive Firemen at Chattanooga, as railway men have always had rather a hold on me.   So I shall not try to address you or say anything beyond thanking you very warmly for your greeting each and all, and [-[illegible]-] say[+ing+] that perhaps those that appeal to me most are the man or woman with a small person in his or her arms[+,+]--I have several of them myself[+,+]--and the man who wears the button of the Grand Army.    I thank you very much for your courtesy and am delighted to see you (Applause).

[note: A short word after "and" is inked out by hand and "ing" is written above to make "saying". The commas before the dashes after "arms" and "myself" appear to be in ink. A small ink tick over the b of "beyond" and above "Army" does not change the words.]

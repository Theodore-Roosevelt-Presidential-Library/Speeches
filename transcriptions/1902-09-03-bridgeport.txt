Mr. Mayor, and you my fellow citizens, my fellow Americans:

I shall detain you but one moment. There was an accident to the party to-day[+,+] to the carriage  I was in[+,+] which resulted in the death of one of the men and another is seriously, perhaps fatally injured. The man who was killed was one whom I was very fond of and whom I highly prized for his loyalty and his faithfulness, and under these circumstances I am sure you will  not mind my not making you a formal address, but [-not-] simply expressing to [-xxx-] all of you my gratitude and appreciation for the wonderful reception that you have given,not me,but the President of the United States. (Applause)   I thank you all with all my heart.   I thank the men of the Grand Army , the men who proved true to a lofty ideal when the days called not merely for work but for risking everything, life itself on the alt[?a?]r of the country.   I thank my younger comrades of the Spanish American War, and above all I wish to express my profound appreciation of the guard of honor,and I say that I can not imagin[?e?] any American President having a more appropriate guard of honor than such a one as I had to-day, representing chiefly the wage-workers of this industrial town.   [-And-] I not only wish to thank them for the courtesy [-xxxx-] that they showed me [-of-][+in+] thus turning out as my guard of honor, but I congratulate them upon the extreme efficiency with which they did their work.   It would be impossible to have a better managed and better handled parade than this has been, and it has been thus handled because of the extreme efficiency with which the guard of honor throughout the [-[?over?]-][+more than+] seven miles kept the road absolutely clear.   I thank you all[+,+] gentlemen.   I thank those of the national guard.  I appreciate very deeply what it has meant [-xx-] thus to be received by you[+,+] men and women, my friends and neighbors, my fellow countrymen, in this way.  I am deeply obliged to you. I know you will not regard me as churlish because under the circumstances I do not make a formal address to you[+;+] and because of those circumstances I shall ask further[+,+] if you are willing, that you do not applaud or make any manifestation of saying goodbye to me. The friend who [-xx-] was killed [-by me-] is now lying unburied[+.+] [-and-] I shall close by simply again expressing to you my profound gratitude and appreciation[+ of your greeting+].

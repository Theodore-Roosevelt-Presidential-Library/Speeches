Ladies and gentlemen:

I {went|want} to thank you very much for letting me say what a pleasure it is to get even a glimpse of you. I only wish I were to stop off here and have a {chan ce|chance} of making more of an address to you. I thank you ladies and gentlemen for having come out to see me. (Applause)

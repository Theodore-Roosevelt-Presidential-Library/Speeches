Gentlemen and Ladies:

I shall not try to talk to you. I shall merely say that I have had a most pleasant fortnight's holiday, and that I have enjoyed so much being in your great and beautiful state. In about a month I shall be back in the western part of it, and I look forward to [-[?returning?]-][+coming+] here again. Good night, and good luck[-[?s?]-] !(Applause)

[note: "returning" is x-ed out on the typewriter; "coming" is typed above it and a hand caret marks the insertion.]
[note: A hand stroke runs through the last letter after "luck" (apparently an "s"). "look" has an overtyped letter.]

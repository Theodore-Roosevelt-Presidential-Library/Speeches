My fellow citizens:

Let me thank you with all my heart for having come here to greet me. I have not [-xx-] enjoyed any meeting more than this. I congratulate you and I congratulate the United States {th at|that} we have such citizens in it! I congratulate you on what you have done here on the farms,in [-[illegible]-] [-[illegible]xx-] business, on all your work, and I congratulate you especially upon the children, upon the way they are being brought up, and upon the way they sing and the tunes they sing. It did me good to see such first class young American citizens here and I am pleased and proud to have [-[?th?]xx-] had the chance of [-[illegible]-]seeing you.

My friends and fellow citizens:

It is a very great pleasure to be  traveling through Idaho . to-day and to have the chance of meeting the men and women of this state. When I get here to the plains and the mountains I feel as if I had come home, for I used to know this country well twenty years ago when I had a cow ranch myself. Yesterday when I went into Butte I [-could[illegible] help [illegible] with-][+met+] one of the mountain men with whom I had been in there fifteen years ago, when we were traveling pretty rough[-[illegible]-][+and+] went to a two-bit restaurant, and I never got a better meal in my life; so I had known the people and had experienced their hospitality and had worked with them and played with them. Naturally I like having the chance [-xxx-] to be back here again. I do not think that most of [-x-] our people understand the  great possibilities that there are in this country. The possibilities are great, notably here where we have a chance for irrigation. But the thing that [-xxxx-] makes me believe in the country is not so much the physical chance as the character of the men and women, [+(Applause)+]

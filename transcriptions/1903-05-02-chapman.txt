[pb 30 425:0339]

My fellow citizens:

I want to say what a pleasure it has been to come through Kansas, to be greeted by the older men who did so well in the great days, and to see the younger[-xxxx-] men [-xxxx-] and be willing to guarantee that they will do as well if ever the need arises. I am glad to see you, men and women and boys and girls. I believe in you with all my heart. I wish you {welle|well.}

[pb 31 425:0339]

[margin: File]

At Chapman, Kansas , the President was presented with a foot-ball. In accepting it he said:

"That shall serve as the text for the sixty seconds I have here. Adopt for life the same rule that I am sure my friend here acts on in the foot-ball field; do not flinch, do not foul, and hit the line hard."

[note: The listed page (left half of frame 0339, folio 30) holds the typed remarks. The facing page (right half, folio 31) is a separate typed sheet, marked "File" by hand, giving his words at Chapman on being presented with a foot-ball; it is included here because it records his remarks at the same stop. Its first two lines are a typed editorial introduction, not his words.]
[note: The last word of the remarks on folio 30 is typed "welle"; the final character may be a period typed over or beside an "e".]

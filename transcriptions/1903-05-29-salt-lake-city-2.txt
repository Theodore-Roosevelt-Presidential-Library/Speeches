[margin: File -- not given out]

I have but one word to say to you. I am very glad to see you. I believe in work and I believe in play. Play hard while you play; and when you work don't play at all. That is good advice for children, and it is good advice for grown folks too. It is a great pleasure to see you. (Cheers and applause)

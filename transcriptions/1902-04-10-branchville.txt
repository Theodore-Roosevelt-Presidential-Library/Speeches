[pb 21 424:0455]

I want to say how very much I have enjoyed my visit down here and how sincerely glad I am to come. You have received me as I knew you would. It has been the greatest possible pleasure and I thank you.

[note: The speech is on the left half (a) of frame 0455, folio 21, below the Summerville remarks of April 9, 1902; the earlier page listing was off. Only the Branchville remarks are transcribed here.]

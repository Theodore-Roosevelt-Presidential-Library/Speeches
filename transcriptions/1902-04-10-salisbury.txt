Ladies and Gentlemen:

As it is growing late I will not attempt to say more than just a word to assure you how much I have enjoyed the hospitality with which I have been received in South Carolina, and this evening since entering the borders of North Carolina.  [-[?Also,?]-] It was my good fortune to meet your Governor yesterday; [+and+] to review a procession in which your[-xx-] state troops took part, and it was a  pleasure as an American to meet my fellow-Americans in that fashion at the Exposition in the old historic [-[?city?]xxxx-] southern city.    I thank you heartily.  I am glad to see you here and I wish you good luck and good night.  (Applause)

[pb 68 425:0360]

My Friends:

It is a great pleasure to be here and to see you. I have been traveling through Colorado all day and have enjoyed to the full getting back here. I believe in Colorado's future; I believe in her mines; in what can be done by irrigation with her fields; in her ranches; but I believe most of all in Colorado's men and women. (Cheers and applause) I like you; I know you, and I think you are all right. (Applause) It does me good to get back into this Rocky Mountain country again. I thank you[-xxxx-] [+very+] much for coming to see me. (Cheers and applause)

[note: Speech is on the left half of the frame. "very" is typed above the line over a word x-ed out on the typewriter. "Friends", "Colorado's" (first) and "to" (in "good to get") are typed over other letters; final readings given.]

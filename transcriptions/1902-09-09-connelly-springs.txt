Gentlemen and Ladies:

I want to say what a pleasure it is to me to be going through your beautiful state this afternoon. I am glad to be here. It is a good thing for any American , and {es ecially|especially} for the American who happens to be President, to go about among his fellow-countrymen throughout this country, and to learn the lesson that a decent American is about the same wherever he happens to live. If he is a good man he is all right. It does not make any difference whether he is in the north or south or east or west, or what his occupation is. All we need to ask is does he do his work squarely as a man and as a citizen, and if he does he is a good American and you can stand by him. (Cheers and applause)

My Friends:

It is a great pleasure to me to be here and see you. It is some sixteen or seventeen years ago that I was last here and the change is wonderful. Now as I pass through it seems literally impossible that the changes could have been so great. I do not wonder that you are proud of this count ry. If you were not I would be ashamed of you. (Applause) I know you; I have worked with you; I have lived with you; and I believe in you with all my heart and soul; and I believe in {thefuture|the future} of this great nation as I do in the future of this state because I believe in the quality of the average citizenship of the state, of the average man and woman who make up the state. (Cheers and applause)

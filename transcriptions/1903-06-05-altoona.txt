[margin: File]

It is a great pleasure to see you,gentlemen. I have just come from a trip across the continent, first to the Pacific, now back again; and the thing that has pleased me most is to find everywhere the substantial unity of Americans. Wherever he goes the President of the United States is at home among his fellow-Americans, east or west, north or south. (Cheers and applause.)

My fellow citizens: I have wanted so much to speak to every one I could in Vermont, but we have made rather too many stops and the train is late - therefore I have time only to say a word of greeting to you.

Labor Day in Vermont! I am glad to speak on that day in this state, for yours is a state whose citizens have always shown that they knew how to work in time of peace and how to work in time of war when the country called (applause). And you have also shown that what you cared to know was whether the man did his work well or ill - not the kind of work it was as long as it was honorable. If a man does decent work of any kind and does it well, honor to him; he is a good citizen. Just as in war time, be the man an officer or an enlisted man, sergeant, lieutenant, corporal, general, if he was bad in his place then you [-did-]do not care to acknowledge the right of comradeship with him . If he did his work well, if he was a good soldier wherever he was, he is entitled to full claim as comrade and brother. So it is in citizenship. Base is the man of wealth who is arrogant because of his wealth, base is the man who envies him. Let us judge each man on his worth as a man (applause.)

Gentlemen and Ladies:

I want to thank you very much for coming to greet me. It is {neary|nearly} 23 years since I first saw what was then the territory of Dakota. I lived most of the time in South Dakota, and I came down along the western edge, along the Black Hills. I was in the cow business myself at that time. I know your people. I am fond of them and proud of them. I believe in them. I have watched[-d-] with the greatest interest your growth to statehood. I thank you for greeting me. I am glad to see the men, the women and the children. I greet you and wish you well and thank you for coming. (Applause)

[margin: not given out]

My friends:

It is a great pleasure to see you and it is a great pleasure to be in Oregon. It has never been my good fortune to be in Oregon before. I know your people well and {fell|feel} thoroughly at home here. I know Oregon's history, I know the men[-x-] and women and what they have done, and I am as glad as I can be to be here to catch [-thx-] a glimpse of your great and beautiful state, and above all of the men and women.in it. I thank you for coming to greet me this evening. (Cheers and applause.)

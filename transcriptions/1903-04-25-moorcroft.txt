Gentlemen and Ladies:

I am glad to have the chance of seeing you. Not far from here I think is the "101" ranch. (Cries of "That's right") I worked with the "101" cowpunchers myself in the old days. I remember perfectly [-[illegible]-] when I lived up on the Little Missouri, that the Belle Fourche here was about the southernmost point we struck [-[illegible]-][?.?] It is a great pleasure to me to get back to Wyoming and greet you. When I get out here in the western Dakotas, Wyoming and Montana I feel that I am back among my old friends.

I am glad to see the pioneers, and I am sure the rest of you will not grudge a special word of greeting to the pioneers who also fought in the great war. It always seemed to me that the men who fought in the great war and the men who won the west by their courage and hardihood, their physical and moral daring, did feats that were fundamentally the same. (Applause)

Gentlemen and Ladies:

I am very glad to see you.   I shall not try to make a speech; just to say that it is a pleasure to be greeted by you and to be here in Kentucky.   I am going down to the Railway Firemen's Convention at Chattanooga, and did not intend to make any addresses on my way down so I shall only repeat my thanks for your having come down.

[note: The "t" of "to see you" is typed over another letter. The left half of the frame holds a handwritten note on White House paper about Cincinnati, Ohio, Sept. 6, 1902, not part of this speech.]

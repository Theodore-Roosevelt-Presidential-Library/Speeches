My fellow-citizens:

I have been delighted to come through your state to-day. I have enjoyed seeing the men and the women and the children.

You have shared in the last six or eight years , even a little more than shared[+,+]in the general prosperity of the nation. This is a state of farmers, and in the southwestern corner,of miners. You have prospered marvelously. Something has been done by laws, but most of all because you have had the stuff in the men who have been tilling the plains, the men who grow the crops and raise the cattle. No law ever was devised that will make a weak man strong,or a clumsy man skillful,or a coward brave. All that any law can do is to give a man a [-xxxxxxxx-] decent chance ; and then he will have to work out his own fate for himself, and if he is of the stock of your people he will work it out all right.

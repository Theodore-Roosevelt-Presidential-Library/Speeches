Gentlemen and Ladies:

I am glad to have the chance of greeting you. I shall say but one word. It is always a pleasure to come here to Wisconsin.  For a number of years I lived further to the north and west, out on the border line of Dakota and Montana, and it is like getting home again to feel the touch of the brisk spring weather and see the fur coat. (Laughter) It reminds me of the days when I punched cows myself;  and I wonder if any of you ever had the experience of accompanying a cattle train.

I am very glad to see you;  I believe in the northwest, for I think that here you are typical Americans.  (Cheers and applause)

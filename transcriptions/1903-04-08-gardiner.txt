My Friends and Fellow Citizens:

It is a great pleasure for me to catch a glimpse of you to-day. Getting back into North Dakota and Montana I have felt that I was at home. I first came to North Dakota twenty-three years ago. I first came to Montana twenty years ago. [-[?I have worked on the range?]-] .I have seen a good deal of your citizens in all kinds of life, and have worked with them in many ways. I believe in you. I am proud of you. I am glad of the wonderful progress you have made and of the way you have built up this state. I wish you all possible luck and success.

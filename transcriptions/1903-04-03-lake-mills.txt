[margin: file]

I will just stop for a moment to greet you and to say how glad I am to be in your state. It is spring, but the fur overcoats are still comfortable. I was fortunate to live a good part of my time a good deal farther west and farther north than this out on the Dakota border, and when I get up where the air has that touch of winter in it, I am at home.     I am mighty glad to see you, I like you, and I am proud of you as fellow Americans. Good-bye.

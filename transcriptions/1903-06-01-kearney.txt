My Friends and Fellow Citizens:

It is a great pleasure to see you here this evening and to be greeted by you. I thank you for coming out. I have enjoyed to the full my trip across [-this country, across-] this continent from one ocean to the other. The thing that has impressed me more than anything else on that journey has been the essential unity of our people, the fact that wherever[-xx-] [+we+] go a good American is at home among his fellow[-s-] Americans. (Cheers and applause) I cannot say how I have enjoyed it. I think I was a pretty good American when I started, and I am going home a better American. I shall not try to say more than just good evening, and to say that I believe in you with all my heart and I believe in the future of the country, because it has men and women like you in it. (Applause)

I shan't make a speech  I am coming back to see Ohio later, {andthis|and this} time I am simply going down to the Firemen's Convention at Chattanooga. I have a pretty good opinion of railway men, and I believe most of you have. All I shall do will be to thank you {wamm ly|warmly} {fw|for} coming here to greet {mep|me,} and I know you will  not grudge [?my?] saying that I prize it most of all in the men who wear the button that shows that they fought  in the great war. Good luck to you. (Applause)

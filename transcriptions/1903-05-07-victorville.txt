My fellow-citizens:

I want to say what a pleasure it is to see you. I am enjoying so much coming into California. I have looked forward toward visiting your wonderful and beautiful state for years, and I am so glad of having the chance of being here. I welcome you all. I am glad to see the men, the women, and especially the children, for I believe {innyour|in your} stock and I am glad it is being kept up. (Cheers and applause.)

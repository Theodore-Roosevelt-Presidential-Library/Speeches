My [?Friends and Fellow Citizens?]:

I am very glad to see you and I am glad to be [-[?here?]-] [+[?back?]+] again in North Dakota, and to be greeted as you are greeting me to-night. It [-[?is?]-] [+[?was?]+] twenty three years ago that I first set foot in North Dakota, and for a number of years I lived my life out in the western part of the state; and I have most naturally a peculiar feeling for [-xxx-] its people. It does me good to see you. You are a great state, a stock-growing state in the west and [-[?still?]-] [+a+] great wheat raising state here; but the reasons I like you most are the men and women, and in addition to the men and women I am glad to see there are a few children down here too . [-[illegible] [?myself. I am an expert?] [illegible]-] You seem to be all right in qua[+[?nt?]+]lity of children and all right in quality, and I am glad of that, because I think it is a good stock and I do not want to see it die out. [-[?N. I intend?] [illegible] [?like to hear the band, so I shall just say good-night, and good luck go with you, and I am mighty glad to have seen you?]-]. ({Chaeers|Cheers} and applause)

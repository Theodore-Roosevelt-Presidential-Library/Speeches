My Fellow Citizens:

It is a great pleasure to come here to-day and to be introduced by Colonel Hepburn, who has been travelling with me through his district. And in parting from it and from him I wish to state my sense of obligation to him and to all the Iowa delegation forthe aid they {gve|gave} me last year-- the invaluable aid in bringing about certain bits of legislation, non-parti[+s+]an in character, which I deemed of the utmost importance; those like a wise supervision {ad|and} regulation of certain great corporations of the type popularly known as trusts, notably of those [-xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx-] engaged in doing an interstate business; legislation which I deemed invaluable not only be cause of its [illegible], but because of its [-xxxxx-] sanity and because it does not [-xxxx-] pretendto do anything that it does not do. A promise should be kept on the stump just as much as off t he stump. The worth of any promise lies in its fulfilment by action, and it was, thanks to Colonel Hepburn, thanks to the Congress, to the members of both the Senate and the House from Iowa and their fellows, that I am able to come before you feeling that all that had been said by us as to the need of such regulation has been made good in fact. Improvements upon the law have been made, better legislation has been put upon the statute books, and the legislation upon the statute books has been enforced with honesty and with fearlessness.

All to-day I have been traveling through Iowa. I have been greeted by Iowa's citizens.Wherever I have stopped there [?have been men?] who fought in the great [-xxxx-] war who have come forward to greet [-xx-] the President. Who has a better right? For there would not be any President to be greeted if you had not [-xxxx-] fought successfully then. (Applause)

Everywhere that I have been I have also seen the school children. I congratulate you upon all you have done in Iowa. I congratulate you upon your crops; but the best crop is.the crop of children. I am glad that is all right in quality, that it is all right in quantity. I believe in you and I want to see your sons and daughters like you. (Great applause)

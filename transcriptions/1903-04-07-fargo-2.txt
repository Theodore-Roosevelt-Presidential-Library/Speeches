My Fellow Citizens:

I shall {n ot|not} try to make any speech to you, but I want to say a word of greeting. I do not feel as if I were a stranger , but rather I feel as if this were more or less a home-coming, for I know your state so well. It is twenty-three years ago that I first came to Fargo, and for a number of years I lived on the western border of the state on the Little Missouri river; indeed I was a very humble county officer for part of that time--I was a deputy sheriff.

It is such a pleasure to meet you here, and to have gotten a glimpse of you. I believe in North Dakota. {Ibelieve|I believe} in its crops, and above all I believe in its men {andwomen|and women}. (Cheers and applause)

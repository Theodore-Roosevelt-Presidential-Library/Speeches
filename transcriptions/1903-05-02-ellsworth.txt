My Fellow Citizens:

As I have passed through Kansas yesterday[-xxxx-] afternoon and to-day I have been struck not merely with the beauty and fertility of your state, not merely with your great material advantages,not merely with the strong, rugged type of your men, but I have been struck also with the great and successful efforts made by Kansas in the cause of education by your colleges, your schools. I am glad of it, particularly for this reason: Kansas is like a blooded horse; Kansas needs education not to develop or stimulate the qualities, but to direct and train them. Kansas is bound to make its weight felt for good or for evil. More and more as I have seen your people I have realized their power, the power in the state, the power of your citizenship, the power of Kansas' manhood and womanhood; and wherever power exists there is a chance of its going wrong. A strong man, because of his strength, if he goes wrong can do worse than a weak man. Kansas made a great record in the civil war. No state has more soldiers, more [-[illegible]-] of those men who won honor in the {t imes|times} that tried men's souls. And you of the younger {generationsent|generation sent} forth many of your number instantly, the minute the call was made upon them; and I would count with absolute certainty upon a regiment from Kansas doing its full share of duty, if not a little more, in case they were needed.

[pb 38 425:0343]

Now having that power means that if that power is not well directed it will be misused. Any country in which there is an abounding virility, abounding manhood, abounding courage, abounding strength, needs to educate and train that strength, that courage, that power, under penalty of seeing it possibly go astray. I {congratulat eyou|congratulate you} and I congratulate the nation upon the educational work {dane|done} in Kansas, because you have the type of citizen which [-if it is a force-][+,+] if it is on the right side[+,+] represents the most potent of forces on the right side, and [-[illegible]-] if it goes wrong[+,+] because of its very strength[+,+] its very power will do an infinity of harm. For that reason especially I feel that the whole country is to be congratulated because of your excellent educational work here,the work of the school, the work of the college, the work of education in its broadest and deepest and truest sense.

There are [-xxxx-][+communities+] which need to educate people in order to bring them up. You do not need that here. What you need here is what any {stwong|strong} and high spirited communit[-ies-][+y+] needs, to be educated in order that it may go right. Just as it is with a horse. Take a perfectly quiet drudge of a horse and train him and he will never turn out very bad or very good either, and take the horse that is best worth riding or driving and the high spirited thoroughbred[+,+] and if he is badly trained he is not an enjoyable animal to handle. Any section of[+the+] people who have the strength, who have the power in [-x-] them[+,+] need to have that power guided and shaped aright, and I congratulate you of Kansas that yours is being guided and shaped aright. I believe in you with all my heart and soul; I am proud of {hyou|you} and prophesy a glorious future for you.(Cheers and applause.)

My friends:

I am glad to have the chance of saying a word of greeting to you. I am thoroughly enjoying my trip through Eastern Tennessee.   I came down here  to attend the meeting of the brotherhood of Locomotive Firemen, but in addition to that it is  a particular pleasure to come here in the battle valley of the  Tennessee,in which the state took its rise-into which the pioneers came even before the Revolutionary War; and I spent most of yesterday going {ofer|over} the Battlefields of Chickamauga and Chattanooga, Missionary Ridge {a d|and} Lookout Mountain. I don't see how any halfway decent  American can come away not feeling better than before.   I am very glad to see you,my fellow countrymen.   (Applause)

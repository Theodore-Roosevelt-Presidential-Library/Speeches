[margin: File]

My fellow Citizens:

I am glad to see you and to {great|greet} you to-day. I have enjoyed passing through your beautiful state. I congratulate you upon all that you raise here in the way of crops, and I {cpngratulate|congratulate} you most upon the children you raise. That crop is all right in quality and quantity too. I have enjoyed thoroughly getting into the state to-day and meeting you, the pioneers and the sons of pioneers who have the upbuilding in this commonwealth. It is a good thing to have men and women able to do the work you have done, and I congratulate you on it. (Cheers and applause)

[pb 72 425:0559]

It is a great pleasure to see you, and I am much obliged to you for coming to greet me. [-I have just come back,-] I passed through here two months and four days ago[+.+] [-and-] I have had a thoroughly enjoyable trip, and I come back in entire safety owing to the railroadmen. There has not been a day and hardly an hour that I have not owed my safety to them, and it is something I appreciate to the full,

I am very glad to see you all. The thing that has struck me most , going from the Atlantic to the Pacific, is the substantial unity of our people, for wherever I have been I have been able to simply speak as one American to his fellow-Americans, and I am among first class Americans now. Good bye and good luck. ( Cheers and applause.)

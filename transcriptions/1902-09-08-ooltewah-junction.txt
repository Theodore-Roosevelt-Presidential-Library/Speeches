My friends and fellow citizens: I shall not try to make any speech to you. I did not know we {wereto|were to} stop here. I have come down only to attend the convention of the Brotherhood of Locomotive Firemen; and so all I shall say is to thank you for your greeting and to express my pleasure at having been here in Tennessee and at having come to Chattanooga, where I saw the great battlefields in which you, my comrade there, took part, and to feel, as I am sure that all of us must feel, that every man who goes over those fields must have a sense of inspiration in the valor shown alike by the men who wore the blue and the men who wore the gray. [+(Applause)+]

[pb 12 424:0688]

I shall not attempt to make a set speech to you. I shall merely say what a pleasure it is to be greeted by you. I am to come back to Ohio.   Now I am merely on my way down to the meeting of the Firemen's Association at Chattanooga.   I have always been rather fond of the railroad men.   I want to thank you for coming , and I know the rest of you won't grudge my saying that I wish to thank particularly the men of the great war,the veterans, for coming to greet me. Good luck go with you.   (Cheers.)

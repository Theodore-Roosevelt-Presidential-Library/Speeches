My fellow-citizens:

I thank you all with all my heart for coming here to greet me. I have been travelling yesterday and to-day {th ough|through} your beautiful state, and I cannot say how I {adm ire|admire} it and how I believe in its future, because I believe in the men and women of the state. . I believe in the men and women of the state, and I believe in the children and am glad to see them and greet them.

It is eminently fitting that here near this old [-xxxx-] United States Military Reservation where we have a portion of our army [-[illegible]-] the President should be greeted by the men of the great war , the men to whom we owe it that there is a [-xxxx-] President or a country to-day. I thank you for your greeting[+.+] [-and-] [-i-][+I+]n Kansas, with its record in the civil war, with its record in the Spanish {Wa r|War} and in the {Philippi ne|Philippine} contest which was its aftermath,I need make no defense of the United States Army. (Applause.) Remember we speak of the volunteer army. Our regulars are volunteers. We have no army except a volunteer army. Officers[+and+]enlisted men in the regular army are our fellow citizens who have volunteered to wear the uniform which is a badge of honor to them and to us, and no body of men in all the country deserve well more emphatically of the entire country than the officers [-xxxx-][+and+] enlisted men of the army of the United States. (Applause.) They have added fresh pages to the honor roll of the republic by what they have done in the philippines, by the [-xxx-] courage and soldierlike efficiency which they have shown in those islands and by the extraordinary moderation, self-restraint and humanity with which they have carried themselves in one of the most difficult and one of the most righteous contests ever waged by any civilized nation.

I am glad [-xxxx-] to have the chance, not merely of greeting you this morning, but of asking you to listen to a few words from one of the ablest public servants with [-[?which?]-][+whom+] any nation at the present time is blest, and as great a War Secretary as any nation has ever had. I [?introduce?] to this audience my friend and your servant, the Hon. Elihu Root, Secretary of War.(Cheers and applause.)

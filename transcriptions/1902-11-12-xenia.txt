I shall not try to make a speech. I shall simply thank you for coming out to greet me and say how glad I am to be met as I have been to-day in passing through your beautiful {s tate|state}. I never pass through it without realizing what an heritage you and I have, and I earnestly hope that each and all of us will resolve to [-[illegible]-][+keep+] that heritage at the high level at which [-was-] it should be. (Applause)

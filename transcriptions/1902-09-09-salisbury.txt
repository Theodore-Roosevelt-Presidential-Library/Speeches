My friends and fellow Americans: I am glad to have the chance of saying even a word to you to-day. It is a good thing for any American to travel through different parts of the country, but it is an especially good thing for the American {whois|who is} President so to do, so as to realise the fundamental unity of our people. I have been particularly struck to-day in going through North Carolina with the signs of your great industrial development. The things that [-[illegible]-][+divide+] one American from his fellow American are very small compared to the things that unite them. A difference of section, a difference of party, is of no concern if we can develop decent citizenship in this country; and [+to do that+] we need [-[?that as?]-][+as+] the foundation - but only as the foundation - [-x-] industrial prosperity What brings it to one part of the country will bring it to [-the other-][+all+]. If you have hard times they do not stop at state lines. If we have a period of prosperity it [-xxxxx-] goes more or less over all the country. It will come to each {secti on|section} if the people of that section see to it that it does come. And that is what you have been doing here. The way you have been building up the industries here in North Carolina, as in Tennessee, is a thing that cannot but rejoice the heart of every American who realizes, as we must if we are wise, that whatever helps one part of the country helps all. If we see North Carolina prosperous, the very fact of that prosperity will ten[+d+] to make the other sections of the country share more or less in it. I welcome the signs of your prosperity not merely because every decent American should be glad [?when?] any of his fellow Americans are prosperous, but because it means [?something?] to all of us. The building up here means something of building [?up?] throughout the country. Something can be done by [-xxx-] good laws. A [?great?] deal can be done against us by bad laws. [-xxx-] Bad laws can prevent [?any?] chance of building up prosperity. Good laws can give us the chance,but they can do no more than that. Good laws can create the condition under which the people themselves can work out for good their own [?salvation?]; but that is all that [?they?] can do. Under a wise system of {legislationthe|legislation the} {opportuhity|opportunity} can be [?given?] for the farmer, the railroad man, the [?manufacturer?], for the [?capitalist?] and the wage worker, for the professional [?man?] and the man who works [pb 57 424:0710] with his hands for his daily bread -- good laws can give to each man of each type of citizenship the chance to develop his {capa city|capacity} to the best advantage. More than that they cannot do. They cannot make capacity, and they cannot [-xxxxx-][+make+] {m|} a man {de velop|develop} it[+.+] But if he has the wish and the power to develop[+ it+] the law can secure the {c onditions|conditions} under which it can be done; but it cannot make the fool wise, it cannot make the knave honest, it cannot make the coward brave. It can shape our system so that wisdom, honesty and courage shall have a chance to get their fair reward. I am glad indeed to be passing through your beautiful country of western North Carolina, as I was in passing through eastern Tennessee yesterday, to see how you are taking advantage of your opportunities, for the opportunity can be turned to advantage only when there [-x-] is the man of brains, the man of perseverance, the man of physical hardihood, who can take advantage of it. I believe in you, I believe in your stock, and therefore I believe that you and those like you are going to make of this region one of the greatest industrial [-x-] centers of all the United States. (Great applause).

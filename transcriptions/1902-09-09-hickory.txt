My [-[illegible]-][+friends+]:

I am glad to have the opportunity of greeting you and being greeted by you. Travelling to-day through North Carolina,yesterday through Tennessee, I have been particularly struck by the evidences of industrial growth of which this town is itself an example. The [-xxxxxxxxxxx-] industrial awakening of the South, of which there have been so many evidences during the past few years is a feature fraught with great benefits to the south and with great benefits to the entire country; (Applause) because, remember, my fellow citizens, we are going to go up or go down together(cries of "That is right!"[+)+] In good times we are all going to feel them; some will {fed|feel} them more than others will; some less; but all will feel the {god|good} times somewhat; and when bad times come we will all be unhappy together.(Laughter) Bad times do not stop at state lines; they do not stop by sections; we go up or we go down together. And the industrial awakening of the south which is going on and which I believe will go on with ever increasing rapidity, means good fortune not merely for the south, but for all the American people. It always happens that a good year for crops is a good year for weeds. When you have prosperity some people for whom you don't much care prosper somewhat too. It is a great deal better that some people should {prosppr|prosper} too much while [-xxxx-] most of us prosper somewhat, than that we should none [-xx-] of us prosper enough-a good deal better to have too much prosperity, even if {accordingmto|according to} the ideas of some of us it is not quite as equally distributed as it should be, than not to have enough {propperity|prosperity} for any one at any time.

The gospel of intelligent hard work is the gospel that pays, and of all gospels the one that pays least is the gospel of envy and rancor, a gospel preached to inflame class against class or section against section.

And so my fellow citizens I hail your industrial growth, I [-hale-][+hail+] the manifestations I see here in this state, as I saw [-xxx-] yesterday in Tennessee, the manifestations of the rapid approach of the period of tremendous industrial development in the south. I [?hail?] it for the sake of the south and I [-hale-][+hail+] it for the sake of the nation.(Cheers and applause)

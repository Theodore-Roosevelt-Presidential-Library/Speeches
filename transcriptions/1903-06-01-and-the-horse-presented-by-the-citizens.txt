[margin: File -- not given out]

Senator Warren; Friends:

I thank you most heartily. I thank my friends of Cheyenne for the beautiful {westernsaddle|western saddle} and bridle. I thank my friends of Douglas for this beautiful horse. I accept them with the greatest pleasure. I am going to take the liberty of re-christening the horse "Wyoming", to commemorate this state; and I shall be proud in Washington, at the capital of the nation, to be riding so fine a [-branded-] [?range?] horse with the saddle and bridle of the cow country--the best that the cow country can produce. I have broken [+in+] the saddle already. I rode the horse yesterday; and I wish to say that I have never been on so {easygaited|easy gaited} a horse who could keep that gait at so good a speed over fairly rough ground. It is like sitting in a rockingchair to ride him; and yet, he has [-xx-] good speed and he is tough and [-hardy.-][+enduring.+] [-I could not have had gifts that would have pleased be more.-] I thank you from my heart. The[-y-] [+horse and saddle+] will serve always to commemorate as pleasant a forty-eight hours as any President has ever passed since the White House was built. (Cheers and applause)

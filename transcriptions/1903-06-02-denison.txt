My Friends and Fellow Citizens:

It is a great pleasure for me to come here and say a word {of|or} two of greeting to you this morning. I only regret that here in the home of Secretary Shaw I cannot stay longer with you. I feel thoroughly at home in Iowa. You see I had to come to this one state to get a quarter of my Cabinet.

In greeting all of you I [-xxxx-] I wish to say a word or two of special thanks to the National Guard who have turned out to-day,-my own comrades -- then to those who ever deserve the right of the line in every American gathering -- the men of the {GrandAmr y|Grand Army} of the Republic, the men to whom we owe it that at the present day an American President can travel from one ocean to the other, from the Gulf to the Canadian border, and everywhere be at home among his fellow Americans.

{Freiends|Friends}, at this time as I come into your beautiful state, [-into your beautiful state-], there have come calamities upon our people here in [-your [?state?]-][+Iowa,+] to an even greater degree in Kansas, in Missouri, I see also by {to-days's|to-day's} paper[+,+] in Georgia. We have {Biblicalauthority|Biblical authority} as well as the authority of common sense for the statement that the rain falls on the just and the unjust alike. When the hand of the Lord is heavy upon any body of men the [-xxxxx-] wisdom of man can do but little. Now and then in our country, from drought, from floods, from pestilence, trouble and misfortune will come; but, oh my friends, as I drove through your city this morning, and now as I look at you, the men and women of this state, I know that all our troubles are temporary; that misfortunes, and we shall have them, {willbe|will be} met and overcome, because in heart and hand and h[+e+]ad the American citizen is able to win his way in the long run. (Applause) When misfortune [-xxxxxx-] that human wisdom cannot avoid comes of course there will be suffering, there will be misery. Those of us who are free from it can try and must try to lighten it all we can, but we cannot help the fact that there will be much suffering.

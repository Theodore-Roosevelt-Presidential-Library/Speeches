Ladies and Gentlemen:

If I had known whom I was to meet here I should have come prepared to take the bath I so urgently need . As it is I shall thank you greatly for giving me this [+chance+] of greeting you. I have enjoyed to the full all of today. I became lured into taking a ride with the cavalry regiment, which accounts for part of my appearance. I did not expect to have the chance of meeting such a gathering, and I shall simply thank you and say how pleased I am to have seen you. I wish you could see me under circumstances more favorable.

Ladies and gentlemen:

I am very glad to catch a glimpse of you this evening. I thank you for having come here to greet me. I have been travelling through South Dakota all day and am now coming to North Dakota, and I feel thoroughly at home.[-xxx-]. I first struck what was then the territory of Dakota twenty-three years ago, so that I can claim to be kind of an old settler myself[-x-]. I live[+d+] a number of years further west,out [-on-] [+in+] the Little [-[illegible]-] [+Missouri+] cow country. It has been a great pleasure to be coming through this country today and seeing the marvelous changes that have been made in the past twenty-three years, and even since I was out here three years ago. I congratulate you upon it and I congratulate the country upon it. The Dakotas have had their full share of the prosperity of the past few years, and they owe it partly to good laws,[-xxxx-] but most[-xx-] especially to the character of the men and women who formed the population. In the long run that is what counts-the stuff the man has in him. (Cheers and applause.)

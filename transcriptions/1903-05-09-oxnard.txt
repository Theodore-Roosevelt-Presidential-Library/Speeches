{Ladi esand|Ladies and} gentlemen:

I wish to say what a great pleasure it had been to be here to-day, and to see the tangible evidence of the extraordinary industry that has been started here within the last five years. It has been the greatest pleasure. I am not surprised at it, because the last two days in California have taught me not to be surprised at anything; [-and-][+And+] [+I am glad+] to see what has been done by your beet {c ulture|culture}, fruit culture of {ev ery|every} kind, irr[?i?]gation, and tilling of the soil[+.+] [-I am not as surprised as I otherwise should be at the astounding success you are making of this country.-] (Applause)

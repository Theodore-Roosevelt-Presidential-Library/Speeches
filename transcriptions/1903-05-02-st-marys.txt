[margin: File]

Ladies and Gentlemen:

I am very glad to catch even this glimpse of you. I greet you and wish you well in your great work of education.

[note: Speech is on the right half of the frame; the Wamego remarks follow it on the same page and are not transcribed here. A faint comma-like mark sits after "your" (possibly a stray or struck comma); not taken as part of the text.]

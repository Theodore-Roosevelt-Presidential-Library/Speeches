Mr. Congressman; Mr. Mayor, and you, My Fellow Citizens:

It is indeed a great pleasure to be in historic Pike County. I have heard a great deal of pike county. I know its fertility; I know its good roads; I know the extraordinary nursery you have got right here, a nursery of a double kind by the way; I passed the new dynamite factory . I congratulate you on all your products but I want to say that what counts finally as the best type of product is the men and women, and therefore, as I like your stock, I want to congratulate you on the children. And one word to them, and it applies just as much to those of an older growth , I believe in play and I believe in work. When you play, play hard, and when you work do not play at all. I have enjoyed greatly coming here into your great and beautiful state. I congratulate you upon the prosperity which we are all enjoying. There will come ups and downs in that prosperity, but on the {wholeas|whole as} sure as there is a sun in Heaven, the tendency will be upward when you have a race like ours standing on a continent as a base. We are sometimes criticised for a vein of boastfulness in talking of the future of our country. {Menand|Men and} women, we cannot overestimate what that future will be if we {areonly|are only} true to ourselves . We must have, as a base of that future as a foundation, material prosperity , material well-being, but that is only the base, only the foundation. Upon it must be {builtas|built as} a superstructure the quality for the lack of which no material well-being can atone , the qualities of [-xxxxxxx-] [-xxxxxxx,-] [+devotion to+] an ideal, of lofty adherence to the right for the right's sake, of rugged power to achieve that ideal in practical methods. It is a fine thing to have a strong body, it is a better thing to have a strong mind, and best of a,ll is it to have that which counts for more than body, for more than mind -- character. In character I include not only the passive virtues, not only the abstention from wrong, but the power to war effectively {fror|for} the right. I include the qualities which made our people alike those that wore the blue and those who wore the gray {leawe|leave} an imperishable heritage of renown for their children and their children's children in the times that tried men's souls. (Applause.)

I am on my way to St. Louis to commemorate the time {acentury|a century} ago when this great country became a part of the United States. I thank you for coming here, I appreciate it to the full, and I wish you for the future an even greater {sshare|share} of the well-being which you now so abundantly enjoy. (Cheers and applause)

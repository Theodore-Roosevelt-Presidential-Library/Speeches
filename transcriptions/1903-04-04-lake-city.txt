Men and Women of Minnesota:

I am glad of having [?the?] chance of seeing you if only for a moment here in your beautiful state;  a state of which you have such cause to be proud, of which the nation has such cause to be proud, for its diversity of agriculture, manufactures, [-the-][+and+] mercantile pursuits, and of which we have most cause to be proud {becauseof|because of} the standard of citizenship {w hich|which} it has; (Applause) the citizenship in peace as the citizenship of you, my comrades there, [-[illegible]-] in [-xxx-].the great war. You did your work well because you {judgedyour|judged your} fellows by their qualities as men, when you moved in battle. [-xxxx-] What you were concerned with about the man on your right hand or your left was not as to his station in life, not as to his wealth; you did not care whether he was a banker or a bricklayer, a farmer or a capitalist, all that you cared to know was that when the pinch came he would stay put. It is just that quality that you want in citizenship, in civil life, [-[illegible]-] in time[-x-] of peace[+—+] [?Not?] genius and brilliancy --you do[-x-] not have too {mubh|much} of them and they are of no use unless you have got a ballast upon them-- you want to know that a man has the commonplace, ordinary qualities that make him a good man in the home, a good neighbor, a friend to be valued, a foe to be feared; [?that?] he does his duty by his neighbor and by the state;  that he will do his duty in the family first -- and if he does not  he is a poor shoat and you cannot do anything with him whatever. (Applause) All of us know the [-[illegible]-][+man+] at the cross-roads who would reform the whole world, but [?he?] does not begin in his own family. [-Xxx-] If you have got [-xx xxxx the-][+a+] man who will do the best he can for those nearest him, for those most dependent upon him, who has the courage, the  honesty, the common sense that makes it pleasant to live next to him,  on the farm and in the shops, in the business community, the qualities good in themselves and the qualities you can use in helping that aggregate of our neighbors which means the state or the nation, you have got a good citizen. But if he has not got these perfectly plain qualities, he is going to be a curse to his neighbors and a curse to the community.

[margin: File]

Gentlemen:

I want to say what a pleasure it is to be greeted by you. I have greatly enjoyed passing through North Carolina. It is a genuine pleasure to come down and see the industrial development that is taking place here[-x-]- The industrial development that is going to make the south one of the most prosperous parts of the Union . (Cheers and {ap plause|applause}.[+)+]

My fellow citizens:

I wish to thank you for giving me this opportunity of saying a word of greeting to you. I am glad to stop here at the seat of the Grant University; [-the-] [+a+] university from which so many men have gone into public  life. It has been the most genuine pleasure to be here in your beautiful country of East Tennessee. I have {bem|been}  glad to see your growing cities. It is a mighty good thing to have manufactories and railroads [-[illegible]-]. It is a better [+t+]hing to have good men and good women. I am glad to see your universities. It is a great thing to have training, intelligence. There is something still more important [-thanxxxxxx-] and that is character/ [-Itxxx-] it is a fine thing to have intellect; it is a finer thing to have character in private and in public, just as you have [-it-] honesty between neighbor and  {neigbor|neighbor}.

(Applause)

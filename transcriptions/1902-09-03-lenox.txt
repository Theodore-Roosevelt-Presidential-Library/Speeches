Ladies and gentlemen:

There has been an accident.   One of the members of the party has been killed; the driver[+,+] I fear[+,+] fatally injured.   Under the circumstances I am sure you will understand my not stopping and not trying to make an address to you.   I thank you heartily for the greeting that you extend to me.   I shall ask you, [-xxxxx-] the circumstances[+ being as they are,+] not to cheer or make any demonstration.   I thank you for coming here to greet me.

{Gentlemenand|Gentlemen and} Ladies:

I thank you very much for coming here to meet me, and I appreciate your greeting greatly. I shall not try to make any extended speech. I am on my way to the Railroad Firemen's Association meeting at Chattanooga, because I like the railroad men and like to go down to their meetings. I shall not try now to speak to you. I thank you again, and I am sure the rest of you will not grudge my saying that always in these meetings I am {pecul iarly|peculiarly} pleased by the presence of the men who wear the button which shows that they were in the great war. {Tey|They} did the big things; we did the {li tle|little} things. Good {luek|luck} to you.

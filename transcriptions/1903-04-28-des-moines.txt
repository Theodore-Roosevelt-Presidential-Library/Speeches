Mr. Chairman; and Ladies and Gentlemen:

When I see this audience here it is a matter of real regret to me that I have not the time to stay and speak to you at length. All I can do, owing to the fact that Iowa has been so very attractive that I have gotten a little behind-hand coming through it, is to say how glad I am to be here to-day. I have enjoyed to the full my trip to-day through your great and beautiful state and my visit now in your beautiful city. I have enjoyed the drive, and I want to say that I have appreciated the quality of the horsemanship of those who have accompanied me. My four immediate guards I would have given a good deal to have [-xx-] had in my regiment. I can say in all sincerity and without any flattery that it does anyone good to travel through your state, because, [-xxxx-] though I am glad to see you with so fertile a soil, though I am glad to learn of your abounding prosperity, and of all that you raise, yet I am most glad at the quality [-xx-] of citizenship that you raise. (Applause) I believe there is no state in the union which surpasses Iowa in the average of happiness of its citizens. You are fortunate in your farming districts and your farming population , in the character of your cities. Iowa is among the leading states of the union in the advances she has made in the training for agricultural life, and of course of recent years the use of the telephone, the use of electric cars, the {introductkon|introduction} of rural free delivery, [-xxxxxxxx xxxxxxxxx xxxxxxxx-] [+have+] made an enormous difference toward equalizing the advantages of [-xxxxxxxxxx-] country and[-xxx-] city life. But glad though I am to get into {citiws|cities} like this, yet I know even the people of the cities will not misinterpret my saying that I am particularly glad to see the farm grow continually more attractive, so that the young [?man?] shall wish to stay on it and stay in the country. We can[?not?] afford to [illegible] do all in our power to keep up the standard of our country pop[?ulation?] .

I want to say how glad I am to be here, how glad I have been to be greeted by your people; and as I like your stock I want to say that I am particularly glad to have been greeted by so many children. (Applause) They seem to be all rightin quality and all right in quantity, and I congratulate you. I believe in you and I want to see others like you come up. (Great applause)

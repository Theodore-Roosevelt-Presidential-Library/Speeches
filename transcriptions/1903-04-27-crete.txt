My friends and {fellow-zitizens|fellow-citizens}:

It is a great pleasure to have the {change|chance} of greeting you to-day. I have enjoyed to the full my trip through Nebraska. I congratulate you upon the prosperity in which we are all sharing. To have it,first of all the hand of the Lord must be with us. Something in addition can be done by laws, by honest [-xxxx-] enforcement of the laws, but after all,in the long run, the thing that counts most is the individual man's character and capacity.(Cheers and applause.) Nothing can take the place of that. Law can do something, but the wisest law ever devised will not give a fool,wisdom, or a weakling strength. I congratulate the people of Nebraska, not only upon their crops and their country, but upon their men and women. (Cheers and applause.)

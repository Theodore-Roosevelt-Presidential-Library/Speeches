Mr. Governor; Mr. Mayor, and Gentlemen:

I shall not try to make a speech. I just had to leave the room to make a speech to some of my fellow citizens in the street. I wish only to say what a genuine pleasure it has been to me to spend the last twenty-four hours in your wonderful and beautiful state, and what a pleasure it has been to me to come to this great state. I have never been in Oregon before, but however you may feel as to me, I have felt thoroughly at home with you. (Applause) I have felt that I knew you, that I felt as you felt, that my ambitions were yours, my ways of looking at things were your ways of looking at them, and I think the fundamental reason is that I hope I am a fairly good American myself and I know I am with Americans than whom none can be better. (Applause)

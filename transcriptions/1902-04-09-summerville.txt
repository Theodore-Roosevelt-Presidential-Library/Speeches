Captain Wagener and Ladies and Gentlemen:

I shall not try to say anything more than to express my obligation to those who for my good fortune brought me down here and my appreciation of the courtesies which have been shown. We have passed two as pleasant days  as it will be possible to pass, Captain Wagener, and it has been to me a double pleasure to come here into the South, into the region from which my mother's people sprang, and to be able, face to face to meet you and  to listen to addresses,Mr. Mayor, like those that you made, that both the present  Governor and Ex-Governor of South Carolina made and that of  the Governor of North Carolina made.

I am not again going to talk about any question of the North and South being reunited; that goes without saying.(Applause) What I shall say is that I only hope you have enjoyed having me here half as much as I have enjoyed being here.    (Applause)

[note: "my" (before "appreciation") is typed over another letter; "courtesies" has a stray mark between "s" and "i". Final readings given.]

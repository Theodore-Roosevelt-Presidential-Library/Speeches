{Ladiesan d|Ladies and} Gentlemen:

I want to say what a pleasure it has been to me to be coming through Colorado to-day. I have visited Colorado before, on business and on pleasure. Now I am going through the state as President, and it has been a very genuine pleasure to come here and to see you,my fellow-citizens. It is a pleasure to see what has been done industrially and agriculturally by your people, to see the upgrowth of cities such as Pueblo, to see what has been done by you in irrigation, and I am thankful to say that now the national government will aid in the irrigation work. While I believe in what you produce , in your crops, in your natural resources, I believe especially in your men and women.(Applause.) [?I?] have absolute confidence in the future of this state for the same reason that I [-[illegible]xxxxx-] have absolute confidence in the future of the nation, because I have absolute confidence in the type of American citizenship that we have here.(Applause.) [-xx-] I wish you well with all my heart. (Applause.)

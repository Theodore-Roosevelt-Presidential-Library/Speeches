My Fellow Citizens:

It is a great pleasure to me to have the chance of greeting you this morning. Late last evening I received a telegram from {Se naotr|Senator} Pritchard, saying I must [-xxxxx-] surely {st op|stop}, if only for a moment,at Marshall. I have been admiring greatly your beautiful scenery coming through this morning. I am glad to see the mountains and the rivers, {b but|but} I am gladder still to see you.(Applause) I have just been down to a {mebting|meeting} at Chattanooga of the Brotherhood of Locomotive Firemen. I have {alway|always} greatly respected the railroad men, because they have certain qualities that I regard as essential to good citizenship.

It always has seemed [-x-] to me a good thing for the President to visit the different parts of the {couhtry|country}, if only to find out that a good American is a good {Apmrican|American} wherever {h e|he} is; and it is of very little consequence whether it is north or south or east or {westnif|west, if} he is a square man and a decent citizen.(Applause)

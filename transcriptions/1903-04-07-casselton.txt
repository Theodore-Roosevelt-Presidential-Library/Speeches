My Fellow Citizens:

It is a great pleasure to me to come here to North Dakota where I feel that I am coming home, for I was here first, twenty-three years ago. For a number of years I lived in the western part of the state; and in those days when I passed through your state it was on a catt[-[?k?]-][+l+]e train as a rule. It is a pleasure to see you. I believe in you; and I wish you well. (Cheers and applause)

My Friends and Neighbors:

I am very glad to see you all. I made up my mind that come what would I would have to have a chance to stop at Medora. I first came to Medora twenty years ago, so I am a middling old settler. I meet boys, great big strapping men, and mothers of families who were [-[illegible]-] [+children+] [-A-][+a+]bout three feet high[-.-] [-W-][+w+]hen I knew them here . It is a very pleasant thing for me to see you. I shall not try to make you but a very short talk, because I want to have the chance to shake hands with you. Most all of you are old friends . I have stopped at your houses and shared your hospitality . With some of the men I have ridden {guardaround|guard around} the cattle at night, worked with them in the round up, and hunted with them, so that I know them pretty well. It is the greatest possible pleasure to me to come back and see how you are getting along,, to see the progress made by the state to see the progress made up at this end in the place that I know so well, and it just does me good to come here and see you; and there is not a human being who is more proud of what you have done, and more pleased with your welfare and progress than I am. (Cheers and applause)

[note: Typed words after "who were" are struck out and "children" is written above them by hand; the period after "high" is struck and the next two capitals are changed by hand to make one sentence. A period after "see you" is circled, and there is a check mark over "with" in "worked with them"; these look like proofreading marks and do not change the words.]

I am not going to make any speech. I am not here on a speech-making trip. I want to thank you and say how glad I have been to see you, and how much I have enjoyed being out with Holt Collier, Pope Swint and all the others who were out with me. ([-Cheersxandxapplausexxxxxxx-] Good night. Good luck. (Cheers and applause)
